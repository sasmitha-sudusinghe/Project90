# Project 90 Master Plan

## 1. Your weekly structure

I recommend this as your default week:

| Day | Main focus | Gym |
|---|---|---|
| Monday | KBS + Computer Security | 🏋️ |
| Tuesday | Mobile Communication + DIP | — |
| Wednesday | KBS practical + Project | 🏋️ |
| Thursday | Computer Security + Mobile practical | — |
| Friday | DIP practical + Project | 🏋️ |
| Saturday | Weekly revision + difficult topics | — |
| Sunday | Light revision + weekly review | 🛌 |

The important part is not studying one subject exclusively for an entire day. You'll repeatedly revisit material, which should help with retention.

## 2. Your typical study day

Don't interpret "3–4 hours" as sitting at your desk for four straight hours.

**Normal university day**

*   **Block 1 — 60 min**: 📖 Learn new theory
*   *10–15 min break*
*   **Block 2 — 60 min**: 🧠 Active recall / questions
*   *30–60 min break*
*   **Block 3 — 60 min**: 💻 Practical coding / problem solving
*   *10–15 min break*
*   **Block 4 — 30–45 min**: 🔄 Revision of something studied earlier

That's approximately 3–3¾ focused hours. On a very busy day, 2 focused hours is better than forcing yourself through a miserable 4-hour session.

## 4. WEEK 1 — OCT 1–4: BUILD THE SYSTEM
Your first few days aren't about going crazy. They're about setting up your entire academic system.

📚 **Academic**
*   **KBS**: Enter all KBS theory chapters into PROJECT 90. Enter all KBS practical topics. Study one introductory theory topic. Complete one practical exercise.
*   **Computer Security**: Enter syllabus. Identify difficult topics. Study first major topic. Create short revision notes.
*   **Mobile Communication**: Enter theory chapters. Enter practical topics. Study first topic.
*   **Digital Image Processing**: Enter theory chapters. Enter practical topics. Identify difficult areas.
*   **Software Quality Assurance**: Enter syllabus. Identify major concepts.
*   **Group Project**: Record project requirements. List your responsibilities. Create project task list. Identify the next concrete deliverable.

🏋️ **Training**
*   Gym Session 1, 2, 3 (Keep the sessions controlled)

🧠 **Discipline**
*   Set October goals
*   Establish sleep/wake routine
*   Create weekly study timetable
*   Complete October starting check-in

## 5. WEEK 2 — OCT 5–11: CONSISTENCY
Now begin your normal academic cycle.

*   **Monday**: KBS (Theory study, Active recall, KBS practical exercise, Gym)
*   **Tuesday**: Mobile + DIP (Mobile theory, Mobile questions, DIP theory, Short revision)
*   **Wednesday**: KBS + Project (KBS practical, Debug/solve practical problems, Group project task, Gym)
*   **Thursday**: Security + Mobile (Security theory, Security active recall, Mobile practical, Review difficult topic)
*   **Friday**: DIP + Project (DIP theory, DIP practical, Project task, Gym)
*   **Saturday**: Revision day (KBS revision, Security revision, Mobile revision, DIP revision, Review difficult topics)
*   **Sunday**: Recovery + planning (Light revision, Review October progress, Plan next week, Weekly PROJECT 90 review)

## 6. WEEK 3 — OCT 12–18: PRACTICAL POWER
This week increases your practical/problem-solving emphasis.

📚 **Academic tasks**
*   **KBS**: Complete 2 practical exercises. Review theory behind each exercise.
*   **Computer Security**: Complete one active-recall session. Solve practice questions. Create a "things I keep forgetting" list.
*   **Mobile Communication**: Complete practical exercise. Revise important theory. Attempt questions without notes.
*   **DIP**: Complete practical exercise. Review underlying concepts. Practise explaining the process from memory.
*   **SQA**: Study one major concept. Create condensed notes. Review previously studied material.
*   **Group Project**: Complete your assigned task. Update project status. Record blockers. Communicate with group.

🏋️ **Gym**
*   Session 1, 2, 3 (Track exercises, sets and reps)

## 7. WEEK 4 — OCT 19–25: CONSOLIDATION
Now start testing whether you actually remember what you've learned. For every subject: Theory recall, Practical exercise, Review mistakes.

*Saturday challenge*: Instead of simply reading notes: Pick one topic from each subject and explain it without looking at your notes. Mark each (🟢 Can explain, 🟡 Partially understand, 🔴 Need to relearn). Put these into PROJECT 90.

## 8. WEEK 5 — OCT 26–31: OCTOBER TEST
This is your first mini assessment of yourself.
Theory self-tests and practical self-tests across all subjects.

## 9. October 31 — PROJECT 90 monthly review
This is important. Don't just ask: "Did I study?" Ask:
📚 **Academic**: How many chapters completed? Improved subjects? Weak subjects? Practical exercises done? Focused study hours? Unexplained topics?
🏋️ **Training**: Gym sessions completed? Performance improved? Proper recovery? Persistent pain/fatigue?
🧠 **Discipline**: Consistency? Distractions? Best study time? Missed sessions? November changes?
*Then create: NOVEMBER — TOP 5 PRIORITIES*

## 10. The most important PROJECT 90 rule
Don't create 50 daily tasks. Your app should have three levels:
*   🔴 **MUST DO**: Your most important academic task + scheduled training/recovery.
*   🟡 **SHOULD DO**: Additional study/revision.
*   🟢 **BONUS**: Extra coding, additional revision, reading, etc.

If you finish your MUST DO tasks on a difficult university day, the day still counts as productive.
